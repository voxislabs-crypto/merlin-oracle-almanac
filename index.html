SOURCE A · TRUTH · LIVE

Kalshi

Contract, strike, expiry, YES/NO, bid/ask, volume, open interest, settlement index and result. Coarse candles are stored. They are not the pattern engine.

SOURCE B · SIGHT · NEXT

Binance

High-resolution OHLCV, swings, candlesticks, RSI/MACD/ATR/EMA/VWAP, Fibonacci math. A hypothesis about the underlying, not Kalshi’s settlement price. Spread monitor gates the two.

SOURCE C · OPTIONAL

Other legitimate feeds

Coinbase, Kraken, TradingView-derived data, CF Benchmarks. Used to test whether a signal exists across the broader market rather than one exchange.
