Fine candlesticks and the TA/Fibonacci math Kalshi’s chart cannot support. This is a hypothesis about the underlying, not Kalshi’s settlement price.

JOIN RULE

A Binance pattern is a hypothesis about where BTC is heading. Kalshi’s contract rules decide what actually settles. Never train on one price and fire on the other without checking the gap.

7 · Discovery, controls, Merlin

PATTERN DISCOVERY

Winners vs the same setup that failed

Do not hunt correlations that confirm the theory. If public profiles show no losses, manufacture the control group: every matching setup where price did not cooperate. Those non-trades are the failed trades.

VALIDATION

A few wins are not a method

Sample size, win/fail rate, mean and median return, max drawdown, variance, confidence intervals, out-of-sample, timeframe and regime sensitivity. Paper trade only after that.

MODELS A / B / C

Merlin stays experimental

A: technical only. B: technical + market structure. C: B + Merlin sky features. Never silently treat astrology as established evidence. Compare whether the extra layer changes performance.
