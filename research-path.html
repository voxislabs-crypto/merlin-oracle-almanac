The path

Kalshi is settlement truth. Binance is the live chart. The spread between them is the gate.

Do not start by asking why a trade won. Reconstruct everything that was knowable at the exact moment of entry. Settlement stays out of the feature vector until after the reconstruction is frozen.

Two jobs, two feeds

Already in the AlmanacYes — client, importer, SQLite vault, UTC, settlementsMilestone 1 — public klines + spread table

Build order

  1. Feed + gate — Binance klines, truth ticks, spread snapshots.
  2. Sight — RSI, MACD, ATR, EMA, VWAP, swings, Fibonacci.
  3. Freeze T — T-60 through T. No settlement in that vector.
  4. Control group — same setup, different outcome. Then Model A / B / C. Paper only after that.
